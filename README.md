📌 Project Overview

The Comprehensive Foods Data Dashboards project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze food products based on their nutritional composition, food categories, vitamins, minerals, and overall health scores.

The project transforms raw food and nutrition data into meaningful visual insights to understand nutritional patterns, compare food categories, and evaluate food quality.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze food products across different categories.

Understand calorie and macronutrient distribution.

Explore vitamins and minerals in different food products.

Compare nutritional values across food categories.

Analyze food health scores and quality.

Identify nutritional patterns through interactive visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.

DAX – Calculated columns, measures, and KPIs.

Excel / CSV – Dataset preparation and data handling.
